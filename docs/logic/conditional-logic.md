# Conditional Logic

## Confirmed conditions
- Countries and Languages appear on Home only when enabled through Home Customizations; they are not enabled by default.
- Global remains selected for Country and cannot be unchecked, deleted or renamed.
- Owner remains selected for Visibility.
- A role with assigned users cannot be deleted until reassignment.
- The protected Owner account and Owner role cannot be removed, blocked, demoted or replaced as specified in Owner Protection.

## Pending Specification
All other visibility conditions, dependent-field rules, empty-state conditions, permission-based component visibility, relationship filtering, conditional requiredness and conditional navigation must be explicitly defined. Do not create inferred conditions.
