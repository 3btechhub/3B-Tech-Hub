# Owner Protection

## Protected Owner account

The primary Owner account is **3btechhub@gmail.com**.

It must never be:

- Deleted
- Blocked
- Demoted
- Replaced

## Protected Owner role

The Owner role must not be deleted or deactivated.

## Role deletion safeguards

A role with assigned users must not be deleted without safe reassignment.

## Default selections

- Owner is checked by default in Visibility.
- Owner cannot be unchecked.

Enforce Owner protections in the interface and trusted backend/database logic.
