# Acceptance Criteria

## Confirmed product-level criteria
- The website exposes Home, Account and Dashboard as its three primary pages.
- Dashboard modules are sections within Dashboard, not standalone pages.
- Home defaults to Service Types, Product Types, Services, Products, Workshops and Technicians.
- The eight catalogue/reference modules require Title and Slug; Users, Roles and Permissions require Email.
- Global Country and Owner Visibility remain protected selections.
- The Owner account and Owner role remain protected.
- A role with assigned users cannot be deleted before reassignment.
- Existing Device Types records and relationships are preserved when presented as Product Types.
- The interface follows the approved mobile-first, English, LTR, Light/Dark and neutral palette direction.
